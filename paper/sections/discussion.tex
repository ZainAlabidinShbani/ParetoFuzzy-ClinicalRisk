\section{Discussion, limitations and threats to validity}
\label{sec:discussion}

%% <<DISCUSSION_RESULTS>>

\paragraph{Statistical power}
Six datasets give little power to across-dataset tests: the smallest attainable
two-sided exact Wilcoxon $p$ is 0.031, and Nemenyi critical differences at $k=11$, $N=6$
are large (Table~\ref{tab:ranks}). We declared this in the preregistration and base
conclusions on effect sizes, per-dataset corrected resampled tests and preregistered
count criteria rather than on across-dataset significance. Even corrected
resampled $t$-tests are approximate, because the 25 folds share training
data~\cite{nadeau2003}.

\paragraph{Datasets}
The benchmarks are public, old, small to medium-sized and convenience-sampled. WDBC and
CKD are near-separable, which compresses discrimination differences (declared in
advance). The thyroid data are imbalanced (6\% positives), so binned ECE is dominated
by the lowest-risk bins. No conclusion about clinical transportability, external
validity or decision utility should be drawn; that requires external validation
cohorts and decision-analytic evaluation~\cite{steyerberg2010,collins2015}.

\paragraph{Design choices}
The development pilot motivated three refinements: an integer literal count, an
external archive, and inner selection of $\lambda$. All of these were fixed before
the held-out data were unlocked. Other choices were not tuned: three fuzzy sets per
variable, $R_{\max}=12$, $L_{\max}=4$, the 80$\times$80 evolutionary budget, and the
$\varepsilon=0.01$ selection rule. The genetic-fuzzy models received the same
evaluation budget as one another but not the same compute as the baselines.
Baselines were tuned with a 20-iteration random search, which may under-tune the
GBDTs relative to larger budgets. Percentile scaling to $[0,1]$ depends on the training
fold, so membership centres are reported in original units for each deployed model.

\paragraph{Interpretability}
Literal counts are a syntactic proxy for interpretability~\cite{gacto2011}. The rule
bases in Section~\ref{sec:rules} are offered as illustrations, not as clinically
validated knowledge. Additive rule contributions can overlap when several rules fire
together, and weights of correlated rules should not be read causally.

\paragraph{Software}
All code, unit tests (leakage, valid probabilities, determinism, Pareto validity,
rule extraction, data integrity), raw per-fold results and the analysis scripts are
released. An automated audit (Section~\ref{sec:protocol}) checks the preregistration
hashes and the provenance of every fit, and regenerates every table and in-text
number from the stored results.
